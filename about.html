About Pune Kabaddi Academy

Pune Kabaddi Academy is a training-focused academy for young kabaddi players in Pune, Maharashtra.

Our Approach

We focus on developing the fundamental skills needed for kabaddi, including raiding, defending, footwork, agility, speed and match awareness.

Training is designed to help young athletes improve their technique, fitness and confidence through regular practice.

Who We Train

Our programs are designed for young players who want to learn kabaddi and develop their skills for school, local and competitive matches.

Our Location

Pune Kabaddi Academy is based in Pune, Maharashtra, and serves young kabaddi players from the surrounding area.
