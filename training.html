Kabaddi Training in Pune

Our kabaddi training program helps young players improve their skills, fitness, speed and match awareness.

Raiding Training

Players practice footwork, hand touches, dodging, balance, timing and raiding techniques.

Defensive Training

Defensive sessions focus on tackling techniques, positioning, reaction speed and teamwork.

Speed and Agility

Players work on acceleration, quick changes of direction, footwork and movement to improve their performance on the mat.

Fitness and Conditioning

Training includes exercises for strength, endurance, mobility and overall athletic conditioning.

Match Preparation

Players practice game situations and team strategies to prepare for school, local and competitive kabaddi matches.
